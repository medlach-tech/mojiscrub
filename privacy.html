Privacy Policy for MojiScrub

Last Updated: May 7th, 2026

This Privacy Policy outlines how MojiScrub ("we," "our," or "the Extension") collects, uses, and safeguards your information. By using this Extension, you consent to the practices described below.

1. Information We Collect

MojiScrub is built with a privacy-first approach. Unlike many extensions, we do not require user accounts.

  • User-Provided Information: None. We do not collect usernames, emails, or IDs.
  • Automatically Collected Data: The Extension stores your "Enabled/Disabled" preferences for specific websites locally in your browser's storage.
  • Page Content: The Extension processes webpage text locally on your device to remove emojis. No page content is ever sent to an external server.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your data is used solely for the following purposes:

  • To remember your scrubbing preferences for specific websites.
  • To provide the core functionality of removing emojis from the DOM.

3. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not share, sell, or trade your data. All processing happens locally within your browser. We do not use third-party tracking or analytics services that collect your browsing history.

4. Data Security

Since your data stays on your device, it is protected by your browser's internal security measures. We recommend keeping your browser updated to the latest version for maximum security.

5. Your Rights

You have full control over your data. You may clear all Extension settings at any time by uninstalling the Extension or clearing your browser's extension storage.

6. Contact

For questions or requests regarding this policy, you can contact the developer at lachmed27@gmail.com.
